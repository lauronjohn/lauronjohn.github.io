**Internship project** · Computer Vision Detection

## The problem

Active construction environments need continuous safety oversight, but human monitoring can't catch every violation in real time.

## Approach

Developed a detection workflow that flags critical safety violations from on-site footage:

1. Collects and prepares multi-dataset training data
2. Trains a detection model (YOLO11m)
3. Deploys detection for on-site safety checks

## Stack

- YOLO11m
- Multi-dataset detection pipeline

## Outcome

Monitored safety harness, smoking, crane, and fence violations — supporting on-site safety checks with automated, real-time detection.
